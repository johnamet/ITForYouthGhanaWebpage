Why organisations work with ITFYG

Because good partnership should create something neither side could do alone.

Organisations can bring resources, expertise, reach, employment pathways and institutional capacity. ITFYG brings youth-centred programme experience, practical delivery, community context and a growing ecosystem around digital skills and opportunity.

How engagement works

Start with the outcome, then design the partnership around it.

The strongest collaborations are usually clearer than “support our work.” They begin with what an organisation wants to make possible and what ITFYG can realistically deliver with integrity.

01
Define the need

Clarify the audience, context, challenge and intended outcome.

02
Shape the model

Agree the programme, partnership, talent or volunteering route that fits.

03
Deliver together

Coordinate implementation, participation, communication and responsibilities.

04
Learn from the result

Use evidence and feedback to understand what worked and what should happen next.
