One mission. Many systems making it possible.

The organisation works as an ecosystem: delivery, evidence, people and partnerships reinforcing one another.
How the organisation works

Departments should not feel like separate offices on a directory.

Each part of ITFYG contributes to the same outcome from a different angle. Programmes and curriculum shape what learners experience. Partnerships and outreach widen access. Monitoring, evaluation and learning create evidence. Operations and people systems make delivery sustainable. Communications helps the work become visible and understood.

The organisation map

Different functions. One connected mission.

This is a conceptual relationship map rather than an organisational hierarchy. It shows how the departments contribute to the same public purpose and why the strongest work happens between departments, not only inside them.

ITFYG mission & learner outcomes
Programmes Turns strategy into learner-facing experiences and programme delivery. Training & Curriculum Shapes what is taught, how it is practised and how learning progresses. Partnerships Connects institutions, resources and opportunity to the work. Community Outreach Brings programmes closer to schools, communities and young people. Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning Creates evidence, feedback and organisational learning. Operations Builds the systems and logistics that let delivery happen reliably. Communications Documents, explains and shares the work with the audiences around it. People & Governance Supports the people, structures and accountability behind the organisation.
