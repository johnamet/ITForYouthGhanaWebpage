Why ITFYG exists

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

We work to close that distance. Through practical technology education, mentorship, community programmes and partnerships, young people gain the skills and support to turn curiosity into something they can use in the real world.

What we do

Different paths. One belief in potential.

Our programmes meet young people at different points in their journey—from first exposure to technology, through deeper training, to entrepreneurship, leadership and real opportunities to move forward.
