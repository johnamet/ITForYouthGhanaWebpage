What we do

We turn access into a path forward.

ITFYG creates practical routes into digital skills, confidence, leadership, enterprise and opportunity—meeting young people at different stages and helping them keep moving.

Explore the programmes See how they connect
Programmes should feel like different chapters of one mission—not a catalogue of disconnected services.
One mission, different pathways

Not every learner needs the same door.

Some young people need their first meaningful encounter with technology. Others are ready for intensive training, portfolio work, entrepreneurship, mentorship or a chance to solve something real. ITFYG's programmes are designed around that progression.

Build Practical skills, projects and confidence.

Girls in Tech

Skill matters. So does seeing yourself in the future you are building.

Girls in Tech creates stronger routes for girls and young women to explore technology, practise publicly, build confidence, find mentors and move into spaces where they have too often been underrepresented.

Representation becomes meaningful when it comes with opportunity.

The programme is not only about getting girls into a room with technology. It is about helping them stay, build, lead and imagine a longer path.

Entrepreneurship Hub

Ideas become stronger when someone helps you test them.

The Entrepreneurship Hub connects digital capability with problem-solving, experimentation, mentorship and the discipline of turning an idea into something other people can use.
