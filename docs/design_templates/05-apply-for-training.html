Before you apply

You do not need to know everything before you begin.

Training is for people who are ready to learn, practise and build. Some applicants arrive with prior experience. Others are beginning with curiosity and commitment. The right starting point depends on the course, your interests and the pathway that fits you best.

Start with fit, not fear.

The application journey should help you understand the available routes and what each one expects before you commit.

What training feels like

Less watching. More doing.

The training experience should be practical and visible. Learners should spend meaningful time applying concepts, receiving feedback, working with others and building evidence of what they can do.

Practical sessions Concepts become clearer when you use them immediately.
Projects Build work that can become part of a portfolio or demonstration of skill.
Facilitator feedback Learn where the work is strong, where it needs revision and why.
Peer learning Ask questions, compare approaches and build confidence alongside others.
Who can apply

Readiness is more than previous experience.

Eligibility should be easy to understand without turning the page into a policy document. Course-specific requirements can remain detailed on the dedicated eligibility page.

Interest You should know why the course or pathway matters to you.
Commitment Training works best when you can participate consistently and complete practical work.
Fit Some courses may have age, schedule or prerequisite requirements.

Where training can lead

The course is not the destination.

Good training should leave a learner with more than attendance. Depending on the programme, the next step may be stronger projects, a portfolio, deeper study, an internship, employment, entrepreneurship or a clearer sense of what to pursue next.
