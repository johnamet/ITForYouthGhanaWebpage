What impact means here

Count the reach. Then ask what changed.

The strongest impact story combines scale with depth. How many young people participated? What did they learn? What could they build afterward? Did they move into another course, a project, work, entrepreneurship, mentorship or leadership? This page should make those relationships visible without pretending every outcome can be reduced to one number.

From access to opportunity

Impact should show a pathway, not just a total.

This concept visualises the logic behind the work: access creates participation, participation creates capability, and capability can open the next opportunity.

Access Young people can enter a learning environment and begin.
Participation Learners practise, collaborate and stay engaged.
Capability Skills become visible through projects and practical work.
Opportunity Learning connects to another course, work, enterprise or leadership.
