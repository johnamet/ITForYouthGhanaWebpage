Prototype notes

Three decisions this prototype settles

1. Advancing a slide keeps the capsule and changes what is inside it

The brief offered three readings: the capsule persists as a stable frame, the whole capsule re-forms on every slide, or the shape morphs between slides. This prototype commits to the persistent frame. The deciding constraint is the one the brief names directly, that it has to hold up when somebody clicks through quickly rather than watching the autoplay. A shell that re-forms makes the silhouette strobe under rapid clicking, and a morphing shell gets interrupted mid-transition and parks in intermediate states that were never designed. A stable stadium reads as one physical object, a lens the organisation is seen through, and the photography changes behind it.

It also buys real usability. The buttons never move, so a fast clicker never has a CTA slide out from under the cursor, and screen-reader focus is never thrown by a re-mounting container. Per-slide identity is carried instead by things that can change without moving geometry: the stage wash built from the existing overlayFrom and overlayTo values, the accent bloom, the rule above the eyebrow, the active pager bar, and the progress ring stroke.

2. The radius system resolves into four named steps, not one

The tokens said 0.375rem and 0.75rem while components reached for rounded-[32px] and rounded-[2rem]. Collapsing everything to the small values would make the capsule impossible, since the capsule language is full-round by definition. Collapsing everything upward would round off buttons and inputs that are meant to read as functional.

So the scale is split by role and every step gets a name: --radius-control at 0.375rem for buttons, inputs and chips, --radius-media at 0.75rem for inline editorial images, --radius-panel at 1.5rem for cards and panels, which is the home the ad-hoc 2rem and 32px values have been missing, and --radius-capsule at 999px for the capsule language itself. Nothing arbitrary survives, and both original intentions are kept.

3. What was kept from the working implementation, and what changed

Kept unchanged: the CMS-driven slide array, six-second autoplay with a visible progress indicator, pause on hover, left and right arrow key navigation, touch swipe, the per-slide overlay colours, the primary and secondary CTA pair, and the splitHeading helper, which in the capsule gets a second job dropping the trailing clause to a lighter tone for a two-beat editorial read.

Kept and repurposed: the blurred background duplicate. It previously existed to fill dead space left by object-contain. Here it becomes the atmospheric stage the capsule floats on, and it is also the layer that still shows every photograph in full. The lens itself crops to a circle with object-cover, because a circular crop reads as deliberate portraiture where a contained photograph inside a circle reads as a mistake. Nothing is silently dropped: the complete frame is still on screen, behind the capsule.

Three deliberate additions. A keyboard-reachable pause control, because hover-to-pause is invisible to keyboard users and autoplay needs a stop under WCAG 2.2.2. Arrow-key handling that ignores events originating in text inputs, so the global listener stops hijacking arrow keys elsewhere on the page. And autoplay that never starts under prefers-reduced-motion, since an auto-advancing carousel is itself the motion being opted out of, leaving manual controls and a fully visible resting state.

No icon components anywhere. Chevrons, the pause and play glyphs, the CTA arrow and the scroll cue are all CSS geometry. No lucide imports, no emoji.

Palette, type and slide copy are taken verbatim from app/globals.css, tailwind.config.ts and the heroSlides seed in lib/content/site-config.ts. Photography is the real repository photography. Class prefixes map one to one onto the components this ports to, so the port is mechanical: SlideshowStage, CapsuleShell, CapsuleMedia, CapsuleContent, CapsuleActions, SlideshowControls, and a headless SlideshowController that can wrap content which is not a hero at all.
