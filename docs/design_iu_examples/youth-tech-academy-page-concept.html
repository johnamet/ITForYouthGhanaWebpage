Overview

A focused pathway with clear outcomes

Cohort-based, project-driven, and built to move participants from their first line of code to a portfolio an employer will actually look at.

The Academy is built around a few commitments that shape everything else: structured, high-accountability training that stays relevant to real life and real work, and a focus on building portfolios rather than simply completing sessions. From there, it supports the transition into internships, jobs, self-employment, or deeper learning, while giving employers and partners a clearer route to reach emerging talent.

Questions

Before you apply

Do I need experience before applying?

No prior technical experience is required for most tracks. The Academy is built to take you from first exposure to a working portfolio over the twelve-week cycle.

What are the four tracks?

Software, design, entrepreneurship, and digital literacy. You're matched to a track after the assessment stage, based on fit and interest rather than prior credentials.

What happens after graduation?

Graduates are supported toward jobs, internships, further learning, or entrepreneurship, depending on where their track and interests point.
