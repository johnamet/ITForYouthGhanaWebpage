IT FOR YOUTH GHANA
Impact Brief 2025

This seeded brief supports the rebuild foundation and demonstrates the report-download

Highlights
- 3000+ youth trained across digital skills and employability pathways
- 8500+ students reached through outreach, clubs, and school activations
- 40% female participation across the wider programme ecosystem
- 85% employment or continued-opportunity signal across tracked learner pathways

What This Brief Is For
- A quick public-facing evidence snapshot
- A lightweight asset for stakeholders who want something downloadable today
- A placeholder structure for richer annual reports and PDFs in later phases

Evidence Themes
- Reach: how many learners, schools, and communities encountered the work
- Inclusion: where the work widened access for girls and underserved learners
- Transition: how learning connected to work, deeper study, or entrepreneurship
- Partnerships: how schools, institutions, and collaborators made outcomes possible
